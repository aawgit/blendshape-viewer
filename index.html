About blendshapes

Blendshapes are built-in changes to a model's shape, often used for facial expressions but also for bodies and other objects. Move a slider to blend in a change; bone animation is separate.

There is no universal naming convention: models may use ARKit, FACS, speech shapes (visemes), or custom names. Recognized ARKit targets appear in the ARKit section; other targets appear under Other model blendshapes. Disabled sliders have no matching target. Models without blendshapes cannot be controlled this way.
