# Test models

Select one of these files under **Upload 3D Model**.

## Microsoft Rocketbox

These busts have 52 ARKit blendshapes:

- `rocketbox-female-adult-01.glb` (813 KiB): [Female Adult 01](https://github.com/miroslavpejic85/mirotalksfu/blob/d35f1141d30ef519c8753a21f0e01dea726bd1dd/public/video-effects/assets/avatars-3d/woman/model.glb).
- `rocketbox-female-adult-11.glb` (727 KiB): [Female Adult 11](https://github.com/miroslavpejic85/mirotalksfu/blob/d35f1141d30ef519c8753a21f0e01dea726bd1dd/public/video-effects/assets/avatars-3d/woman-2/model.glb).
- `rocketbox-female-adult-12.glb` (766 KiB): [Female Adult 12](https://github.com/miroslavpejic85/mirotalksfu/blob/d35f1141d30ef519c8753a21f0e01dea726bd1dd/public/video-effects/assets/avatars-3d/woman-4/model.glb).
- `rocketbox-female-adult-15.glb` (878 KiB): [Female Adult 15](https://github.com/miroslavpejic85/mirotalksfu/blob/d35f1141d30ef519c8753a21f0e01dea726bd1dd/public/video-effects/assets/avatars-3d/woman-3/model.glb).
- `rocketbox-male-adult-01.glb` (744 KiB): [Male Adult 01](https://github.com/miroslavpejic85/mirotalksfu/blob/d35f1141d30ef519c8753a21f0e01dea726bd1dd/public/video-effects/assets/avatars-3d/man/model.glb).

These are derived from [Microsoft Rocketbox](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox) models and converted to GLB by [MiroTalk SFU](https://github.com/miroslavpejic85/mirotalksfu). The conversions include cropping to a bust, renaming blendshapes, and meshopt compression. See the [upstream asset license](https://github.com/miroslavpejic85/mirotalksfu/blob/d35f1141d30ef519c8753a21f0e01dea726bd1dd/public/video-effects/assets/avatars-3d/LICENSE.md).

## Other sources

- `talkinghead-mpfb.glb` (35.1 MiB): [MPFB avatar](https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead/blob/1563de184c19b310de4b636664d5835e2c74dbba/avatars/mpfb.glb), created with Blender and MPFB, from [TalkingHead](https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead/blob/1563de184c19b310de4b636664d5835e2c74dbba/README.md). This textured full-body model has ARKit and additional viseme targets. Reduce **Scale** to 1 to see the full avatar; its face remains small in the viewer. The upstream credits identify this asset as [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `vitruvian-head.glb` (9.7 MiB): [Vitruvian head](https://github.com/ibrews/VitruvianGodot/blob/8b643c3e72f39cb2f775ddeeac1612f70a9e1606/godot_project/vitruvian_head.glb), from [VitruvianGodot](https://github.com/ibrews/VitruvianGodot/blob/8b643c3e72f39cb2f775ddeeac1612f70a9e1606/NOTICE.md), based on work by Sean Buckley, Olaf Delgado-Friedrichs, and Upliner. This standalone head renders without hair or visible texture in the viewer. Its targets use non-ARKit names, so use **Other model blendshapes**. The upstream notice identifies the head and character textures as [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `talkinghead-brunette.glb` (4.5 MiB): [Brunette avatar](https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead/blob/1563de184c19b310de4b636664d5835e2c74dbba/avatars/brunette.glb) by Ready Player Me, via [TalkingHead](https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead/blob/1563de184c19b310de4b636664d5835e2c74dbba/README.md). The unmodified full-body model includes ARKit and viseme targets. The upstream credits license this asset under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): attribution is required and use is non-commercial only.
- `visage-female.glb` (9.9 MiB): [Female demo avatar](https://github.com/readyplayerme/visage/blob/1349f4863dc116ed8f2e9706bccb1246e0bd304d/public/female.glb) by Ready Player Me. The unmodified full-body model includes ARKit and viseme targets. It is published in Ready Player Me's [MIT-licensed Visage repository](https://github.com/readyplayerme/visage/blob/1349f4863dc116ed8f2e9706bccb1246e0bd304d/LICENSE); no separate asset-specific license is stated there.

The two Ready Player Me models above have been inspected for embedded blendshapes but have not been tested in the viewer.
